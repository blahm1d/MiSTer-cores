Simple download method

Download downloader_blahm1d.ini:
https://github.com/blahm1d/MiSTer-cores/releases/latest/download/downloader_blahm1d.ini

Place it in your MiSTer's SD card root, then run Update_All.
You'll find my cores under Arcade → blahm1d.

To get new releases and updates, run Update_All again.
Leave Update_All's Arcade ROMs option enabled for ROM downloads.

Includes Gladiator, The Legend of Kage, Wolf Unit, Y Unit, X Unit / Revolution X,
T Unit, Exidy 440 and NARC. Some builds are beta; current build details and known
issues are at https://github.com/blahm1d/MiSTer-cores/blob/main/RELEASES.md.
