Simple download method

Download downloader_blahm1d.ini (right-click, Save link as):
https://mister.blahm1d.com/downloader_blahm1d.ini

Place it in your MiSTer's SD card root, then run Update_All.
You'll find my cores under Arcade → blahm1d.

To get new releases and updates, run Update_All again.
Leave Update_All's Arcade ROMs option enabled for ROM downloads.

Includes Cruis'n USA, Gladiator, The Legend of Kage, Night Slashers, Wolf Unit,
Y Unit, X Unit / Revolution X, T Unit, Exidy 440 and NARC. Some builds are beta;
current build details and known issues are at https://mister.blahm1d.com/RELEASES.md
